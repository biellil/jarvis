**Step 5 — Test the voice pipeline end-to-end (ARCH-05 success criteria):**

- Electron console shows:

  - `[voice-handler] Sending to LLM gateway...`
  - `[voice-handler] Synthesizing TTS via murf` (or elevenlabs)
