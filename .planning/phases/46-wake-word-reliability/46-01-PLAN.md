<objective>
Fix the root cause of missed "Hey JARVIS" activations: a sign inversion in the mel spectrogram normalization formula, and restore test coverage so regressions are caught automatically.

Purpose: The mel normalization formula `x / 10 - 2` shifts every value fed to the openwakeword embedding model outside its training distribution by 4 units, causing the classifier to produce scores near 0.0001 for all audio — including real wake word utterances. Changing to `x / 10 + 2` (the formula from openwakeword/utils.py) restores correct distribution and enables the classifier to detect the wake word. The broken test environment (all 8 tests fail with `ReferenceError: document is not defined`) also needs to be fixed so the normalization change is immediately verified by CI.

Output: One-line fix in WakeWordEngine.ts + test environment fix + updated mock sessions → all 8 tests green.
</objective>
